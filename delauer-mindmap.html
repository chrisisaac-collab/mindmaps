1 Scoop Fades Wrinkles, Brightens Skin & Reverses Biological Age

Thomas DeLauer · Nutrition & Longevity · June 2026

🧬 Collagen Science Basics ▶
▶ What Collagen Is
Collagen is the most abundant protein in the human body, forming roughly 30% of total protein content. It forms the structural scaffolding of skin, tendons, ligaments, and bones. Production declines at roughly 1-1.5% per year from our mid-20s, accelerating after 40 — making supplementation increasingly relevant with age.
▶ Types of Collagen
Type I collagen dominates skin, bone, and tendons and is most relevant for anti-aging skin benefits. Type II supports cartilage and joints. Type III works alongside Type I in skin and vascular tissue. Hydrolysed collagen peptides are broken into short amino acid chains for superior absorption.
▶ Why Supplementation Works
When collagen peptides are digested, specific dipeptides (hydroxyproline-proline) reach the dermis and act as signalling molecules, stimulating fibroblasts to produce new collagen and hyaluronic acid. This is a documented mechanism - not just passive amino acid delivery.
▶ Absorption & Bioavailability
Hydrolysed collagen peptides have a molecular weight of around 2-5 kDa, small enough for intestinal absorption. Studies show collagen-specific peptides appear in blood within 1 hour of ingestion and accumulate in skin tissue within 12 hours of regular supplementation.
✨ Skin & Wrinkle Benefits ▶
▶ Wrinkle Reduction Evidence
Clinical trials show 6-12 weeks of daily collagen peptide supplementation reduces skin wrinkle volume by up to 46%, wrinkle area by 44%, and wrinkle depth by 9% compared to placebo. These represent genuine, measurable structural improvements in the skin - not marginal changes.
▶ Skin Hydration
Collagen peptides stimulate hyaluronic acid synthesis in the dermis. Hyaluronic acid holds up to 1,000x its weight in water, directly increasing skin moisture content. Studies report up to 34% improvement in skin moisturisation after 6 weeks of consistent collagen supplementation.
▶ Skin Brightness & Tone
Collagen supplementation reduces melanin irregularities by improving skin cell turnover and reducing oxidative stress in the dermis. The glycine content also supports glutathione synthesis - the body's master antioxidant - which directly influences skin luminosity and evenness.
▶ Elasticity & Firmness
Collagen peptides stimulate elastin production alongside new collagen fibres, restoring skin elasticity lost through aging. Elasticity decline - the socalled snap-back quality of youthful skin - is directly addressable through consistent supplementation.
Biological Age Reversal ▶
▶ What Biological Age Measures
Biological age reflects the functional state of your cells and tissues - distinct from chronological age. Key markers include DNA methylation patterns (epigenetic clocks), telomere length, and inflammatory biomarkers.
▶ Collagen & Epigenetic Clocks
A 2025 clinical trial in npj Aging found that oral collagen supplementation reduced biological age by 1.4 years within 6 months via epigenetic clock algorithms.
▶ Anti-Inflammatory Pathway
Glycine inhibits NF-kB and reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines including TNF-alpha and IL-1beta, which drive accelerated biological aging.
▶ Realistic Expectations
1.4 years over 6 months is meaningful but modest. Collagen works best as part of a broader anti-aging stack combined with adequate sleep, resistance training, and dietary quality.
Optimising Your Protocol ▶
▶ Dosage & Timing
10-15g of hydrolysed collagen peptides daily, taken with vitamin C. Consistency over at least 8-12 weeks required.
▶ Vitamin C as Cofactor
Without ascorbic acid, the body cannot complete the hydroxylation step of collagen synthesis. Take 200-500mg with your collagen dose.
▶ Source Quality Matters
Marine collagen has superior bioavailability due to smaller peptide size. Grass-fed bovine is the next best. Avoid low-dose gummies and capsules which rarely reach therapeutic thresholds.
▶ Synergistic Nutrients
Znc, copper, silica, and hyaluronic acid amplify structural benefits. Zinc and copper are cofactors for lysyl oxidase, the enzyme that cross-links collagen fibres.
Collagen Beyond Skin ▶
▶ Joint & Cartilage
Randomised trials show reduced joint pain and improved mobility after 24 weeks, particularly for those over 40 with exercise-related joint stress.
▶ Gut Lining
Glycine and proline support tight junction integrity in the gut wall, reducing intestinal permeability.
▶ Sleep Benefits
3g of glycine before bed reduces core body temperature and improves sleep quality and daytime alertness per multiple RCTs.
What DeLauer Cautions Against ▶
▶ Overhyped Marketing
The evidence base is largely from industry-funded trials covering 8-24 week windows. Long-term data beyond 2 years is still limited.
▶ Dose Matters
Most gummies contain 1-3g - well below the 10-15g therapeutic threshold. Check the label for actual peptide content.
▶ Sunscreen Comes First
UV radiation accounts for up to 80% of visible facial aging. SPF 30+ daily delivers more anti-aging protection than any supplement stack.
▶ Not a Replacement
Smoking, poor sleep, excess sugar, and chronic stress degrade collagen faster than a supplement can replace it.
