Building Your AI DNA

A repeatable system for personalising AI to work for you

🧬 What Is "AI DNA"?
▼
🔹The Core Concept
Your "AI DNA" is a structured, personal data set you feed into AI so it understands your voice, values, goals, and context. Rather than starting every prompt from scratch, your AI DNA lets the model respond as if it already knows you deeply — producing far more relevant, personalised outputs from the very first message.
🔹Why Generic AI Fails
Most people use AI like a search engine — typing short, context-free queries and getting generic answers. Without personal context, even the most powerful model can only deliver average results. AI DNA solves this by turning a blank-slate tool into a personalised strategic partner.
🔹The "Clone" Metaphor
Igor frames AI DNA as the foundation for building an "AI Clone" — a version of AI trained so thoroughly on who you are that it can think, write, and decide in your style. This clone doesn't replace you; it multiplies your output by handling the tasks that drain your time and energy.
🔹From Working With AI to AI Working For You
The 2026 Bootcamp theme reflects a maturation in how people use AI. Year 1 taught you to work alongside AI. Year 2 is about deploying an AI that runs tasks autonomously in the background — scheduling, drafting, researching — while you focus on higher-leverage work.
🥪 The Context Sandwich Framework
▼
🔹Layer 1 — Who You Are
The first layer of the Context Sandwich is your identity: your name, background, industry, role, values, and communication style. This anchors every subsequent AI interaction in your real-world context, so outputs don't just sound smart — they sound like you and apply directly to your situation.
🔹Layer 2 — The Task
The middle layer is the specific task or question you need help with. Because it's sandwiched between rich personal context and a clear output spec, the task gets handled with far more nuance than a standalone prompt. The AI doesn't just answer the question — it answers it for you, in your world.
🔹Layer 3 — The Output Format
The third layer specifies exactly what you want back: a bullet list, an email, a 200-word social post, a structured plan. This removes guesswork for the AI and ensures you get something immediately usable rather than something you need to edit heavily before applying.
🔹Why Sandwiching Works
Context Sandwich is a replicable structure, not a one-off trick. Once you build your personal context block, you paste it into the top of any AI conversation and instantly transform a generic tool into a personalised assistant. Igor calls this "the shift from prompting to programming your AI."
🎯 Dean's Goal Acceleration Method
▼
🔹90-Day Goal Setting
Dean emphasises breaking big annual goals into focused 90-day sprints. A goal that's too large creates paralysis; one that's too small creates complacency. The 90-day window is short enough to feel urgent, long enough to create real momentum — and small enough to feed into an AI that can track and support your progress.
🔹The 7 Levels Deep Exercise
Dean uses a recursive "why" drill — asking yourself why your goal matters, then why that answer matters, seven layers deep. By the seventh level, you typically surface an emotional truth (e.g., "I want to break a cycle my children will inherit"). This emotional root becomes part of your AI DNA, helping AI generate content that actually motivates you.
🔹Stretch Goals and the Brain
Dean encourages setting goals that are slightly beyond what feels achievable, because a near-impossible goal forces your brain to explore solutions it wouldn't consider for a comfortable target. AI becomes a powerful partner here — it can generate strategies for stretch scenarios your rational mind would dismiss without data to back them up.
🔹AI as Your Goal Accelerator
Once your goals are embedded in your AI DNA, the model can proactively identify constraints, generate possible solutions, draft accountability messages, and remind you of your "why" when you're stuck. Dean demonstrated live how asking AI "What are my top three constraints right now?" against your DNA context can surface blind spots within seconds.
🤖 The AI Clone — Building & Training
▼
🔹The Onboarding Prompt
Igor walks through a master "onboarding prompt" that positions AI as your personal strategic advisor, efficiency expert, creative partner, and execution engine — all in one. This prompt sets expectations for every future interaction: the AI treats every task with the mindset of a world-class operator who understands your specific business and goals.
🔹Training on Your Voice
A key step in building your AI Clone is feeding it examples of your actual writing — emails, social posts, sales scripts, even voice memos transcribed to text. The more examples you provide, the more accurately the AI can replicate your tone, vocabulary, and cadence across any type of content.
🔹Teaching It How You Think
Beyond voice, Igor introduces the idea of teaching AI your decision-making framework — how you prioritise, what trade-offs you prefer, how you handle risk. When AI knows your thinking model, it can draft recommendations that feel like they came from you, not from a generic advisor with no skin in the game.
🔹Automation and Scaling
The final stage is automating tasks so the clone runs without your active input: drafting your weekly newsletter, responding to common enquiries, updating project trackers, or generating content from raw notes. Igor shows how the 2026 Bootcamp specifically addresses this autonomous layer that didn't exist in last year's program.

Together, these three layers transform a blank-slate AI interaction into a personalised session.

The key insight Igor drives home is that your personal context block only needs to be written once. After that, you paste it into the top of any AI conversation and the model is instantly oriented to your world. This is the shift from "prompting" — which is laborious and inconsistent — to "programming your AI" — which is systematic and scalable. 01

Write Your Personal Context Block Today

Draft a 200–400 word document describing who you are: your name, role, industry, target audience, communication style, top values, and current goals. Paste this at the top of every AI conversation this week and notice how dramatically the output quality improves — even before you refine it further.

02

Use the Context Sandwich Structure for Every Prompt

Stop writing one-line prompts. Structure every AI request in three parts: (1) your personal context, (2) the specific task, (3) the exact output format you want. This alone will move your results from generic to immediately usable within a single session.

03

Do the 7 Levels Deep Exercise on Your #1 Goal

Pick your most important current goal and ask yourself "why does this matter?" seven times, building on each answer. Record your final answer — this is your emotional root. Add it to your AI DNA so the model understands not just what you want to achieve, but why it matters to you at a core level.

04

Set a 90-Day AI Goal, Not a Vague Annual One

Convert your annual ambitions into a single 90-day sprint goal. Make it specific, slightly uncomfortable, and measurable. Feed it into AI with your personal context and ask: "What are my top three constraints to achieving this in 90 days?" Use the answer to plan your week.

05

Use the Master Onboarding Prompt to Initialise Every Session

Copy Igor's onboarding prompt structure: position AI as your "personal strategic advisor, efficiency expert, creative partner, and execution engine." Tell it to treat every request with the mindset of a world-class operator who understands your specific business and goals. This single prompt transforms the default AI persona into a high-leverage collaborator.

06

Feed AI Real Examples of Your Writing to Train Your Voice

Gather 5–10 examples of your best writing — emails, posts, scripts, proposals — and paste them into a new AI session with the instruction: "Here are examples of how I write. From now on, match this tone and style." The more examples you provide, the more accurately AI can replicate your voice in future content tasks.

07

Use the ELI5 Method to Eliminate Research Drag

Whenever you encounter a complex document, contract, technical report, or dense article, upload it to AI and ask: "Explain this to me like I'm five — what are the three most important things I need to understand?" This takes minutes and eliminates hours of research or expensive specialist consultations for basic comprehension tasks.

08

Identify Your Top Three "Background Tasks" for Automation

List every task you do repeatedly each week that involves writing, summarising, researching, or formatting. Pick the top three by time cost. These are your first automation targets. Write a clear brief for each and start delegating them to AI with your personal context block pre-loaded — you'll reclaim hours within the first week.

09

Ask AI for an Uncomfortable Truth About Your Current Approach

Once your AI DNA is loaded, try Igor's power prompt: "Based on everything you know about me, what is one uncomfortable truth about my current approach that I need to hear right now?" This prompt leverages your personal context to surface blind spots that a generic AI query would never reach — use it monthly as a strategic check-in.

10

Treat AI Literacy as a Compounding Investment

Every hour you invest in building your AI DNA, refining your prompts, and training your Clone compounds over time. The participants who saved 15+ hours a week in Year 1 did so because they built the system in Month 1 and then let it run. Start building your system today — even a rough version — rather than waiting until you have the perfect setup.
